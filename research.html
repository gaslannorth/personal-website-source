Research

01 · Primary theme

Unstable slopes and slope processes

I investigate slow-moving landslides and deep-seated gravitational slope deformation by relating displacement time series to slope geometry, temporal forcing, and geomorphological setting.

Focus Kinematic segmentation · geological controls · hydrological forcing · seismic triggering

Selected work

Landslide mapping and monitoring using PSI in the French Alps2020 ↗ Transient motion of the largest landslide on Earth2021 ↗ Kinematic segmentation of the Leo Pargil DSGSD using InSAR2026 ↗

02 · Primary theme

Arctic ground dynamics

I study rock-glacier motion, permafrost-related deformation, freeze-thaw processes, and climate-sensitive slope instability in Svalbard, Jan Mayen, and Arctic Norway.

Focus Permafrost · rock glaciers · freeze-thaw · climate-sensitive terrain

Selected work

InSAR Svalbard Ground Motion Service2026 ↗ Earthquake-triggered rock avalanche on Jan Mayen2026 ↗

Connected themes

Related questions extend from aquifer systems and active faults to regional monitoring services.

03

Subsidence and hydrogeological deformation

I examine deformation associated with groundwater extraction, geothermal production, urban development, and water infrastructure using long InSAR time series and hydrogeological information.

Subsidence in the Bursa Plain2019 ↗ Ground deformation in Cape Coral, Florida2023 ↗

04

Tectonic deformation and fault processes

I investigate aseismic fault creep, interseismic deformation, and earthquake-related ground response using InSAR together with GNSS, seismic, and geological observations.

Shallow creep along the İzmit earthquake rupture2019 ↗ Earthquake swarm at Sørfjorden, Norway2026 ↗

05

Ground-motion monitoring and InSAR services

I contribute to the development, validation, and interpretation of regional InSAR services, including InSAR Norway and InSAR Svalbard, for systematic ground-motion monitoring.

Methods and implementation

Methods used across themes

Across these themes, I combine InSAR time-series analysis with terrain interpretation, acquisition geometry, GNSS, and site-specific evidence. I also develop ITAS to support more effective use of InSAR in slope-process investigations.
